# Signed-out landing review · 2026-10-10

These ten live home pages were reviewed for their visible information hierarchy and page structure. They are references, not templates or endorsements. Ngombe uses its own copy and bundled farm photo.

| Page | Observed structure | Relevant lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) | Clear promise, product demonstrations, customer stories, then a final action. | Show a concrete workflow after the hero; avoid invented customer metrics. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) | A focused proposition followed by a recognizable product interface and deeper workflows. | Let the record workflow carry the visual proof. |
| [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) | Short promise, grouped use cases, then customer/logo proof. | Keep the promise concise; do not show partner logos without a relationship. |
| [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/) | Prominent action followed by sections for distinct jobs. | Put sign-in in the hero and separate daily jobs below it. |
| [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) | Visual product examples and routes into specific tasks. | Use small, concrete task examples instead of another generic icon grid. |
| [AgriWebb](https://www.agriwebb.com/) | Livestock-specific promise, farm imagery, record benefits and producer testimony. | Lead with real farm work; do not invent testimonials or scale claims. |
| [Farmbrite](https://www.farmbrite.com/) | Farm-specific headline, action, then task and record sections. | Speak to the work between milkings rather than broad software language. |
| [FarmKeep](https://www.farmkeep.com/) | Plain farm-management promise, product image and attributed customer stories. | Show what the app records; defer reviews until there are consented users. |
| [Patagonia](https://eu.patagonia.com/gb/en/home/) | Strong photography and editorial stories beyond a product grid. | Give the farm photo room and use an editorial rhythm. |
| [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/) | Immediate primary task and focused category navigation. | Make the existing-account action easy to find without claiming registration exists. |

## Applied here

- One bundled farm photograph, a direct sign-in link, and two distinct stories about animal records and milking sessions.
- A static three-session illustration, explicitly separate from real farm data. No auto-playing carousel or third-party image requests.
- Existing capability summary retained, with no fabricated partner names, reviews, usage statistics or cloud/offline promise.
- Reduced-motion and reduced-data preferences respected; signed-out farm views stay gated by the existing application logic.
